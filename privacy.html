Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who we are

Wanted Level is a fan-run GTA VI clips project. "Wanted Level Uploader" (the "App") is our internal desktop tool that uploads videos we edited to YouTube and TikTok accounts that we own. Contact: wantedlevel.canais@gmail.com.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Wanted Level channels, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the App accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the basic information of our own channel (youtube.readonly), used to confirm which channel is connected.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to confirm which account is connected, and permission to send our videos to that account's drafts (video.upload) or publish them (video.publish).

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other person, viewer or streamer account.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file, and are used only to upload our own videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and websites). Deleting the local token files also removes the App's access. To request removal of any content or for questions, write to wantedlevel.canais@gmail.com.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
